Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Sarkari form ke liye exact 20KB se 50KB photo kaise banayein? ▼

"Passport & Signature" tab par jaayein, apni photo upload karein, preset mein "India Standard (3.5x4.5cm)" chunein aur Target KB mein "50" dalein. Button dabate hi system automatically photo crop karega aur 50KB ke andar file de dega.

Mobile phone par ise app ki tarah kaise chalayein? ▼

Website ko Chrome mein open karein aur top right par 3 dots (⋮) par tap karke "Install App" ya "Add to Home Screen" dabayein. iPhone par Safari ke Share icon par tap karke "Add to Home Screen" chunein.

Kya PDF to PowerPoint conversion free hai? ▼

Ji haan! 100% free hai, bina kisi login, subscription ya watermark ke. Har slide high-resolution 16:9 widescreen format mein convert hoti hai.
